About

weekform turns a week of training into one image: seven circles, Monday to Sunday, showing what you did each day. Runs, rides, swims, gym sessions, sport, yoga, rest days.

Tap a day to add what you did, then share the image. It works without an account.

Why

Made because I wanted it for myself: a way to share my training with group chats without flooding them with every workout.

Who made it

Built by amiablealex as a personal project. The source is at github.com/amiablealex/weekform.
