Privacy

Without an account

Your weeks stay in your browser and in the link. Nothing is uploaded. The image is made on your device.

One number is counted when a strip is shared: that it happened. No address, no device, no contents.

With an account

Stored: your email address, a hash of your password, the weeks you save, any goals you set, and any preset weeks you keep.

All of it is stored as it arrives and is not read, analysed or profiled. Whether a goal is met is worked out in your browser and is never sent anywhere or kept.

Why: so you can reach your own history from more than one device. That is the only reason.

Never sold. Never shared. No advertising. No tracking. No analytics.

Others involved

Railway hosts the site and its database. Resend sends password reset emails — the only email sent. Google serves the fonts, and sees the request.

Keeping and deleting

Data is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account removes the account, every saved week, every goal and every preset at once, with nothing retained in a backup.

You can download everything held about you at any time, from Settings.
